CB-SX-01

Slatwall Double Wire + PVC Price Tag

Twin arms with a stepped lower wire carrying a clear PVC label holder.

Two arms carry the product and a shorter lower wire carries the ticket. Because the tag rides below the facing rather than in front of it, price stays legible with the peg full and the front unit stays fully visible — the layout most planograms are drawn against.
