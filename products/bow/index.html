Grid Hooks — Bow Clip

Bow-form grid clip for welded wire mesh up to 80 × 80 mm aperture.

7 stock styles Wire grid / mesh panel

Load spread across the mesh, not one weld.

The heavier of our two wire-grid backs. A bow-shaped 1.5–2 mm clip wraps two mesh wires at once and spreads load across the grid rather than pointing it at a single weld — the right call for dense, heavy or high-turn planograms on large-aperture mesh.

Fits
Welded wire grid, apertures up to 80 × 80 mm
Mounting back
25 × 83 mm bow clip, 1.5–2 mm
Part prefix
WJ-SG-
Finishes
Chrome, nickel, zinc, powder coat

Typical applications

  • Wire-grid shop-in-shop walls
  • Cash-and-carry and wholesale mesh bays
  • Trade show and exhibition grids
  • Back-of-house and stockroom mesh
