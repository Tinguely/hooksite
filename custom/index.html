Custom & OEM / ODM

Bring a drawing. Or a sample. Or a photo of the shelf.

Eighteen years of bespoke hook work for global buyers. We will work from a technical drawing, a physical sample, or nothing more than a picture of the fixture and a description of what has to hang on it.

What we customise

Four things change. The engineering does not.

01

Draw it to your spec

Dimensions, shape and load capacity worked from your drawing, a physical sample, or a photo of the fixture it has to live on.

02

Finish it to your brand

Chrome, nickel and zinc plating, or powder coat in any RAL or Pantone — matched to the fixture, not to what happens to be on the line.

03

Pack it as your own

Private label, custom carding, poly-bagging and carton counts. The hook can arrive ready for your shelf, under your name.

04

Prove it before you commit

Fast prototyping and in-house mould development, so a small trial run can be proved out before it becomes a container.

Tooling and prototyping

Prove it small before you ship it big.

Mould development happens in-house, so a new geometry does not sit in an external tool shop’s queue. That is what makes a genuine trial run possible.

  • Fast prototyping from drawing, sample or photograph
  • In-house mould and tool development
  • Small-batch trial production before committing to volume
  • Scale from trial run to full container without re-tooling

Finishing

Brand-matched, not batch-matched.

Powder coat to any RAL or Pantone, matt or gloss, so a shop-in-shop build reads as one fixture rather than four suppliers.

Chrome

Bright mirror finish. The retail default — reads clean under store lighting and hides handling marks.

Nickel

Softer, warmer lustre with strong corrosion resistance. Common on premium and optical fixtures.

Zinc

Sacrificial galvanic protection. The value option for stockroom, back-of-house and damp environments.

Powder coated

Any RAL or Pantone colour, matt or gloss. Used for brand-matched fixtures and shop-in-shop builds.

Private label

It can arrive as your product.

Custom carding, poly-bagging, printed cartons and your own part numbering. For distributors and fixture houses, the hook can land ready for the shelf under your name.

  • Private-label carding and header cards
  • Poly-bagged multi-packs to your count
  • Printed cartons with your SKU and barcode
  • Carton quantities set to your pick-and-pack process

Next step

Send us the drawing.

Dimensions, load case, finish, annual volume — or just a photo and a description. We will tell you what it costs and how fast we can prove it.
