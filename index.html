How We Work

Distribution & Ordering

We operate as a wholesale distributor. All orders are fulfilled through our standard process below. Minimum order quantities apply per product line.

Step 01

Submit Inquiry

Send us your product requirements, quantities, and destination via the contact form or email. Include any custom specifications.

Step 02

Receive Quote

We provide an itemized quote including unit pricing, lead time, and shipping estimates. Samples available upon request for qualifying orders.

Step 03

Confirm & Pay

Orders are confirmed with a purchase order. Payment terms: 30% deposit, balance prior to shipment. T/T, wire transfer accepted.

Step 04

Ship & Track

Standard production lead time: 15-30 days. Shipment by sea (LCL/FCL) or air. Full documentation and tracking provided.

Minimum Orders

MOQs vary by product line. Standard hooks typically start at 500 units per SKU. Contact us for mixed-SKU pallet options and first-order accommodations.

Shipping & Customs

We ship FOB and CIF. Full commercial invoice, packing list, and certificate of origin provided. We can assist with HTS classification upon request.

Custom Production

Custom sizing, branding, and packaging available for orders meeting production minimums. OEM/ODM services offered through our manufacturing partner.
