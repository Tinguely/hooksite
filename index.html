How We Work

Distribution & Ordering

We operate as a wholesale distributor. All orders are fulfilled through our standard process below. Minimum order quantities apply per product line.

Step 01

Submit Inquiry

Send us your product requirements, quantities, preferred finish, and destination via the contact form or email. Include any custom specifications.

Step 02

Receive Quote

We respond within 2 hours. Your itemized quote includes unit pricing (FOB/CIF), production lead time, and freight estimates. Samples available for qualifying orders.

Step 03

Confirm & Pay

Orders confirmed with purchase order. Payment by T/T (wire transfer) or Western Union. 30% deposit to confirm production; balance before shipment.

Step 04

Ship & Track

Shipped FOB, CIF, EXW, or Express Delivery. Full documentation: commercial invoice, packing list, and certificate of origin. Tracking provided on dispatch.

Minimum Orders

MOQs vary by product line: 50 pcs (adjustable hooks), 200 pcs (jewelry hooks), 500 pcs (slatwall / pegboard), 1,000–3,000 pcs (standard wire hooks). Mixed-SKU pallet options available — contact us to discuss.

Shipping & Terms

Available Incoterms: FOB, CIF, EXW, Express Delivery. Main export markets: South America, Western & Eastern Europe, Eastern Asia. Full commercial documentation provided on all shipments.

Custom Production

Custom wire diameter, hook length, finish color, and ball-end configuration available. OEM / private label packaging offered for orders meeting production minimums. Lead time typically 15–30 days.
