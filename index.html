What we actually do

A hook is the cheapest part of the fixture and the first one to fail.

When an arm sags, a clip walks out of the groove or a ticket holder snaps, the cost is never the hook. It is the re-merchandising labour, the lost facing and the shrink.

We have spent eighteen years on that single problem. Clip geometry stamped in-house, arms formed on CNC wire machines, every joint welded on dedicated equipment, and four finishes specified against the environment the hook has to survive — not against what happens to be running that week.

Surface finish

Four finishes, specified against the environment.

Every part in the catalogue is available in all four. Powder coat matches any RAL or Pantone for brand-built fixtures.

Chrome

Bright mirror finish. The retail default — reads clean under store lighting and hides handling marks.

Nickel

Softer, warmer lustre with strong corrosion resistance. Common on premium and optical fixtures.

Zinc

Sacrificial galvanic protection. The value option for stockroom, back-of-house and damp environments.

Powder coated

Any RAL or Pantone colour, matt or gloss. Used for brand-matched fixtures and shop-in-shop builds.

Manufacturing

Ninety machines. One roof.

Stamping, wire forming, welding, finishing, assembly and packing all happen in the same building in Zhejiang. Nothing is subcontracted, so nothing waits in someone else’s queue.

  • 30+ stamping machines blanking clips, ticket plates and sole plates from coil
  • 30+ CNC wire formers running arm geometry to repeatable bend radii
  • 30+ dedicated welders on the arm-to-clip joint — the one that actually fails
  • 1,500–2,000 m² of raw material and finished-goods warehousing

Inside the factory

From coil to carton

01

Raw material

Steel wire and sheet held in stock by gauge. Buying in depth is what lets us quote a lead time honestly instead of quoting the mill’s.

02

Stamping

Clip bodies, price-tag plates and mounting backs blanked and formed on 30+ presses against hardened tooling.

03

Wire forming

Automatic formers cut and bend the arm — length, U-loop, wave, ball stub — to the drawing, run after run.

04

Welding

Arm-to-clip and ball-to-stub joints welded on dedicated machines, then pull-checked before the batch moves on.

Custom & OEM

If it is not in the catalogue, it is a drawing away.

Roughly half of what leaves the factory is made to a customer’s specification rather than ours — different lengths, different load cases, brand colours, private-label packaging.

  • Draw it to your spec — dimensions, shape and load capacity worked from your drawing, a physical sample, or a photo of the fixture it has to live on
  • Finish it to your brand — chrome, nickel and zinc plating, or powder coat in any ral or pantone — matched to the fixture, not to what happens to be on the line
  • Pack it as your own — private label, custom carding, poly-bagging and carton counts. the hook can arrive ready for your shelf, under your name
  • Prove it before you commit — fast prototyping and in-house mould development, so a small trial run can be proved out before it becomes a container
