What is actually on the floor.

Stamping machines

30+

Wire forming machines

Process

Coil to carton, in seven stages.

Nothing leaves the building between the raw wire and the packed carton, which is why a lead time we quote is a lead time we control.

01

Raw material

Steel wire and sheet held in stock by gauge. Buying in depth is what lets us quote a lead time honestly instead of quoting the mill’s.

02

Stamping

Clip bodies, price-tag plates and mounting backs blanked and formed on 30+ presses against hardened tooling.

03

Wire forming

Automatic formers cut and bend the arm — length, U-loop, wave, ball stub — to the drawing, run after run.

04

Welding

Arm-to-clip and ball-to-stub joints welded on dedicated machines, then pull-checked before the batch moves on.

05

Surface finish

Chrome, nickel, zinc or powder coat to your specification, including brand-matched RAL and Pantone colours.

06

QC and assembly

Dimensional and finish inspection, price-tag holder fitting, and any private-label carding or bagging.

07

Packing and despatch

Cartoned to your count, private-labelled if required, and staged for consolidation or direct container loading.

Quality

Quality first, integrity-based operation, continuous improvement.

Not a slogan on a wall — a full-process control system that runs from raw material procurement through to finished goods despatch.

  • Incoming inspection on wire gauge and sheet thickness before anything is cut
  • Dimensional checks against the drawing at forming and again after welding
  • Pull-testing on welded joints, the failure point that matters most in service
  • Finish inspection for coverage, adhesion and colour match before packing
